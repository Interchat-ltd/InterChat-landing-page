Communities

  • Roles in a community Owners, admins and moderators keep a community running well.
  • Prayer requests and "I prayed" Share a need, even anonymously, and let others respond that they've prayed.
  • Polls Ask your community a question and see how people vote.
  • Pinned messages Admins can pin a message so it stays easy to find.
