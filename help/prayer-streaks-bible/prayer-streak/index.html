How does my prayer streak work?

Your prayer streak counts the days you've shown up consistently for your own time with God. A day counts toward your streak when you complete all three of these:

  1. Read today's verse (the Verse of the Day).
  2. Finish a guided prayer.
  3. Pray for someone on the Prayer Wall.

All three need to be done on the same day for it to count — doing just one or two won't keep the streak going.

Your prayer streak is personal to you. If you'd like to keep a streak together with a friend instead, see Faith streaks with a friend, which works a little differently.
