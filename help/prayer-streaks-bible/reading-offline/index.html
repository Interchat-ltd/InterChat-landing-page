Reading the Bible offline

InterChat saves the chapters you've recently read on your phone, so you can keep reading them without an internet connection.

This works for translations whose publishers allow offline reading — Bible translations on InterChat are licensed from their publishers, and some licences don't permit saving content for offline use. If a translation can't be saved offline, you'll need a connection to read it.

Offline chapters are kept on your device only, and are removed if you delete the app.

For content beyond reading — like audio Bibles, sermons and podcasts — see Listening to audio content.
