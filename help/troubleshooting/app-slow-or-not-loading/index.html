The app is slow or not loading

If InterChat is running slowly, stuck, or not loading, try these in order:

  1. Check your internet connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to see if one works better.
  2. Close and reopen the app. This clears up most temporary glitches.
  3. Restart your phone, if closing the app doesn't help.
  4. Check for an update in the App Store or Google Play. An outdated version can behave unexpectedly.
  5. Check your storage. A phone that's nearly full can make every app, including InterChat, run slowly.

If none of this helps, contact support@interchathq.com with your app version and phone model — in the app, Settings → About → Contact support fills these in for you automatically.
