Prayer Rooms aren't recorded

Prayer Rooms are not recorded by InterChat. They're live voice gatherings, connected through our calling provider, and nothing said in them is stored by us afterwards.

We ask the same of everyone using InterChat: please don't record or rebroadcast a Prayer Room without the agreement of everyone present. Our Community Guidelines treat Prayer Rooms as a sacred space of trust, and screenshotting, recording or sharing someone's prayer to embarrass or expose them is never allowed.

If someone does record or share a Prayer Room without permission, you can report it — see Reporting and what happens next.
