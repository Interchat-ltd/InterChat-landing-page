Prayer Rooms

Live voice gatherings where your community prays together.

  • What is a Prayer Room? A live voice gathering inside a community, where members take turns to pray.
  • Joining and taking turns You join muted, raise your hand to pray, then unmute when it's your turn.
  • For hosts: managing a Prayer Room Mute, manage turns, remove someone, or end the room.
  • Scheduling a Prayer Room Plan one ahead, reply Going or Can't make it, and add it to your calendar.
  • Prayer Rooms aren't recorded InterChat doesn't record them, and you shouldn't either.
