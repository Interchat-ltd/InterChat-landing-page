Privacy Policies

Welcome

InterChat is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, including messaging, video calls, social features, and related services.

We respect your personal information and are transparent about how we handle it. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

1. Overview

InterChat collects information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our Services. This includes:

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Profile information (bio, photos, interests)
  • Communication data (messages, calls, shared content)
  • Technical information (device type, IP address, usage data)
  • Location data (with your permission)

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways:

  • Information you provide directly (registration, profile setup)
  • Information automatically collected (usage patterns, device data)
  • Information from third parties (with your consent)

2.1 Information You Provide

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and other details.

Profile Information: You may provide a profile picture, bio, interests, and other optional information.

Communication Data: All messages, voice recordings, and video calls are stored securely.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information

Usage Data:

  • Features you use and how often
  • Time and duration of sessions
  • Search queries and interactions

Location Information:

  • Approximate location (country, city)
  • GPS coordinates (only with explicit permission)
  • Inferred location based on IP address

3. How We Use Information We Collect

We use collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining InterChat Services
  • Personalizing your experience
  • Communicating with you about updates and support
  • Improving platform security and preventing abuse
  • Analyzing usage patterns and improving features
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Marketing and promotional activities (with consent)

3.1 Processors and Third Parties

We may share information with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, payment processing)
  • Law enforcement (when legally required)
  • Business partners (with your consent)
  • Other users (as part of your profile and communications)

4. End-to-End Encryption

InterChat uses end-to-end encryption for direct messages and voice calls. This means:

  • Only you and the recipient can read encrypted messages
  • InterChat cannot access message content
  • Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Encryption keys are stored securely on your device

5. How We Share Information

We share information only when necessary:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our rights and user safety
  • With service providers under confidentiality agreements
  • In case of business transfer or acquisition

5.1 With Other Users

When you use InterChat, certain information is visible to other users:

  • Your profile name and photo
  • Your bio and status
  • Messages and shared content
  • Your online/offline status

5.2 Service Providers

We work with trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics services
  • Payment processors
  • Customer support tools
  • Email delivery services

6. Legal Compliance

We may disclose information when required by law or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect the rights, privacy, and safety of InterChat and users
  • Respond to lawful government requests

7. Impression Services

InterChat may use analytics services to understand how users interact with our platform. This helps us:

  • Improve user experience
  • Track feature usage
  • Identify technical issues
  • Understand user demographics and preferences

8. AI and Future Technologies

InterChat may use AI for:

  • Content moderation and spam detection
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Automated customer support
  • Security threat detection

We will disclose AI usage and obtain user consent where required by law.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide Services:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Messages: Stored according to your settings
  • Analytics data: Aggregated and deleted after 12 months
  • Legal holds: Retained as required by law

10. Your Rights and Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Disable location tracking
  • Object to certain data processing

11. International Data Transfers

InterChat may transfer your information internationally. When we do:

  • We ensure adequate safeguards are in place
  • We comply with data protection laws
  • We use Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable
  • We obtain your explicit consent for transfers outside your region

12. Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for messages
  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure password storage with hashing
  • Regular security audits
  • Multi-factor authentication support
  • Access controls and data minimization

13. Children's Privacy

InterChat is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

Parents who believe their child has provided information should contact us immediately.

14. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand usage patterns
  • Improve user experience
  • Detect fraud and enhance security
  • Deliver targeted advertising (with consent)

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

15. U.S. and California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have rights under CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what data is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing of data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to limit use of sensitive information

16. GDPR, UK GDPR and Global Privacy Rights

If you're in the EU, UK, or other jurisdictions with strong privacy laws:

  • You have the right to access your data
  • You have the right to be forgotten
  • You have the right to data portability
  • You have the right to object to processing
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with authorities

17. Brazil (LGPD) Rights

Under Brazil's LGPD, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Opt out of profiling
  • Revoke consent for data processing

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated to you via email or prominent notification. Your continued use of InterChat after changes constitutes acceptance.
