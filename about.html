Why we built InterChat

Faith, scattered across a dozen apps.

Believers already live out their faith online — in WhatsApp groups that move too fast to keep up with, church announcements that get buried, Bible apps that don't talk to each other, and prayer chains that quietly fade out. The conversation, the Scripture and the prayer are all there. They're just scattered.

We built InterChat to bring them back into one place — a safe, focused home where you can chat with your community, pray for and with each other, and grow in your walk with God, without losing any of it to a dozen different apps.

How we keep InterChat safe

Safety isn't an afterthought.

  • Clear community guidelines that apply everywhere on InterChat, enforced consistently.

  • Reporting and blocking are built into every chat, community, story and prayer request.

  • Zero tolerance for anything that endangers a child — read our Child Safety Standards.

  • We don't sell your data. Read exactly what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy.
