## Common questions

### Account and sign-in

**I didn't get my verification code.**
Wait a minute, then tap **Resend code**. Check your spam or junk folder. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly; you can change it from the code screen.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email, then the 6-digit code we send, then a new password.

**Why do you ask for my date of birth?**
InterChat is for people aged 13 and older, so we confirm your age when you sign up.

**How do I sign out of a lost phone?**
On another device, go to **Settings → Where you're signed in** and sign that phone out. Then change your password.

**How do I delete my account?**
See [Delete your account](delete__user.html).

### Chats and communities

**How do I find people I know?**
Tap **Find people you know** on Chats. If you allow contact sync, we'll show which of your contacts use InterChat. You can also search by name or username.

**How do I join a community?**
Go to **Updates → Communities → See all**, or use an invite link someone shares with you (interchathq.com/join/…). Private communities need an admin to approve you.

**How do I start a community?**
Tap the new chat button → **New community**, pick a template (Bible study, Prayer group, Church or Friends) or start from scratch, and invite people.

**How do I mute a chat or community?**
Swipe left on it in Chats and tap **Mute**, or open its info. You'll still be notified when someone @mentions you.

**How do I block or report someone?**
Open their profile → ⋯ → **Block** or **Report**. To report a message, long-press it → **Report**.

### Prayer Rooms

**What is a Prayer Room?**
A live voice gathering inside a community, where members take turns to pray. You join muted and unmute when it's your turn. Prayer Rooms aren't recorded.

**I didn't hear the Prayer Room ring.**
Check **Settings → Notifications → Prayer Rooms**, and that the community isn't muted. On Android 14 and later, allow **Full-screen notifications** for InterChat. On some phones (such as Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi and Oppo), also allow **Show on lock screen** and **Pop-up windows** for InterChat in your phone's settings.

### Notifications

**I'm not getting notifications.**
1. Check that notifications are allowed for InterChat in your phone's settings.
2. In the app, open **Settings → Notifications** and tap **Send a test notification**.
3. Check that **Quiet hours** aren't on and the chat isn't muted.
4. On Android, turn off battery optimisation for InterChat.

### Streaks

**How does my prayer streak work?**
A day counts when you complete all three daily goals: read today's verse, finish a guided prayer, and pray for someone on the Prayer Wall.

**How does a faith streak with a friend work?**
A day counts when you both share a verse, quote, sermon, podcast or reading plan with each other as a card. Texts, photos, voice notes and prayers don't count.

### InterChat Plus

**How do I cancel?**
In the app, go to **Settings → InterChat Plus → Manage subscription**, or cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

**I changed phones and lost Plus.**
Sign in with the same account and tap **Restore purchases** on the Plus screen.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. Request one through the App Store ([reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)) or Google Play (your order history).

### Bible

**Can I read offline?**
The last chapters you've read are saved on your phone for offline reading, for translations whose publishers allow it.
