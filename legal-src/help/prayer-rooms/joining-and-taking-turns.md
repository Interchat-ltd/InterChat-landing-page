When you join a Prayer Room, you join **muted** by default, so the room stays calm and focused as people arrive.

To pray out loud:

1. **Raise your hand** ✋ to let the host know you'd like to pray.
2. Wait for your turn.
3. **Unmute** when it's your turn to speak, then mute yourself again once you're done.

This keeps prayers focused one at a time, rather than everyone speaking at once. If the room is guided around a particular purpose — praying for a specific need, for example — it helps to keep what you share in line with that focus.

For what hosts and co-hosts can do to keep things running smoothly, see [For hosts: managing a Prayer Room](/help/prayer-rooms/host-controls/).
