---
title: Scheduling a Prayer Room
summary: Plan one ahead, reply Going or Can't make it, and add it to your calendar.
keywords: schedule prayer room, reminder, going, can't make it, add to calendar
order: 4
---

Rather than starting a Prayer Room on the spot, you can schedule one ahead of time so your community knows when to show up.

Once a Prayer Room is scheduled, members can reply **Going** or **Can't make it**, so the host has a sense of who to expect.

You can also **Add to calendar**, which creates a link like `interchathq.com/prayer-room/…` — opening it later takes you straight to the Prayer Room's lobby when it's time, whether or not InterChat is already installed on the device you open it from.

Scheduling is a good way to set up a recurring prayer meeting or a one-off gathering around something specific your community wants to pray about together.
