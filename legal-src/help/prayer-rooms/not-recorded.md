---
title: Prayer Rooms aren't recorded
summary: InterChat doesn't record them, and you shouldn't either.
keywords: recording, privacy, prayer room recording
order: 5
---

Prayer Rooms are **not recorded by InterChat**. They're live voice gatherings, connected through our calling provider, and nothing said in them is stored by us afterwards.

We ask the same of everyone using InterChat: please don't record or rebroadcast a Prayer Room without the agreement of everyone present. Our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html) treat Prayer Rooms as a sacred space of trust, and screenshotting, recording or sharing someone's prayer to embarrass or expose them is never allowed.

If someone does record or share a Prayer Room without permission, you can report it — see [Reporting and what happens next](/help/privacy-safety/reporting/).
