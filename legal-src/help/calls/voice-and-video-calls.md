You can start a voice or video call directly from a chat with someone on InterChat.

Calls are connected through our calling provider and need a working internet connection — they'll use mobile data if you're not on Wi-Fi, so it's worth being mindful of your data plan for longer calls. See [Using less data](/help/troubleshooting/using-less-data/) if that's a concern.

**Calls aren't recorded by InterChat.** The same privacy care that applies to Prayer Rooms applies here.

**Important:** InterChat isn't a replacement for a phone service, and it **can't be used to call emergency services**. If you or someone else needs urgent help, contact your local emergency services directly.

Your call history (who called whom, when, and how long) is kept until you delete your account — see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) for the detail.
