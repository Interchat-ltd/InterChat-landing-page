title: Posting a story

A story is a text, photo or video update that's visible to others for a limited time, rather than staying in your profile permanently.

**Who can see it** is controlled by your story privacy settings, the same place you manage who sees your profile photo, bio and last-seen status. You choose the audience when you post, or ahead of time in your settings.

**How long it lasts.** A story is visible for **24 hours**, then it's automatically removed from view. You can delete a story yourself before then, if you'd rather take it down early.

**Viewers.** You can see a list of who has viewed your story, and anyone who views a story can react or reply to it.

Posting a story is a good way to share a testimony, an encouragement, or a moment from your day with the people who follow you, without it needing to be a permanent post.
