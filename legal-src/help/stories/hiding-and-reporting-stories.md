---
title: Hiding or reporting someone's stories
summary: Quietly stop seeing them, or report one that breaks the guidelines.
keywords: hide stories, mute stories, report story
order: 2
---

If you'd rather not see a particular person's stories, you can **hide or mute their stories** without blocking or unfollowing them outright — they won't know you've done it.

If a story breaks InterChat's [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html) — for example, it's harassing, hateful or unsafe — you can report it instead:

1. Open the story.
2. Tap **⋯** → **Report story**.

Reports are confidential — the person who posted it isn't told who reported them. For what happens after you report something, see [Reporting and what happens next](/help/privacy-safety/reporting/).

If the story is from someone you'd rather not interact with at all, consider [blocking them](/help/privacy-safety/blocking/) instead, which also stops them seeing your stories.
