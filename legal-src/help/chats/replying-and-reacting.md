title: Replying, reacting and @mentions
summary: Respond to a specific message without losing the thread.

In a busy chat or community, it helps to respond to a specific message rather than just adding a new one at the bottom.

- **Reply** to a message to quote it, so it's clear what you're responding to.
- **React** to a message with an emoji — a quick way to say you saw it, agree, or send encouragement without writing anything.
- **@mention** someone by name to make sure they notice your message, even in a muted chat. Muting a chat doesn't stop you being notified when someone @mentions you — see [Muting a chat or community](/help/chats/muting-a-chat/).

These work the same way in one-to-one chats and in communities, making it easier to keep a conversation readable even when a lot of people are talking at once.
