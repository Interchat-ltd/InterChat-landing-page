title: Deleting a message
summary: Delete for yourself, or for everyone — and what each one leaves behind.

---

You can only delete messages you sent yourself. There are two ways to do it:

**Delete for me.** Removes the message from your own view only. Everyone else in the chat still sees it.

**Delete for everyone.** Removes the message from view for everyone in the chat, straight away. It's worth knowing that a copy may still be kept for a limited time for safety and legal reasons — for example, if the message had already been reported before you deleted it.

Deleting a message doesn't notify the other person with a special alert, but depending on how the chat displays it, they may see that a message was removed.

If you'd rather remove an entire conversation, or a story, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#how-long-we-keep-information) for how long different kinds of content are kept.
