Alongside text, you can send several kinds of content in any one-to-one chat or community on InterChat:

- **Photos and videos**
- **Documents and files**
- **Voice notes** — press and hold the microphone to record, then release to send
- **Shared verses, quotes, sermons and reading plans** as cards
- **Polls**, for getting a quick answer from a group
- **Reactions and @mentions** on other people's messages

Everything you send belongs to you — see our [Terms](/terms.html) for exactly what permission you give InterChat to host and deliver it.

A couple of things worth knowing: InterChat processes message content (including photos and voice notes) to do things like show notification previews and respond to reports, so it isn't end-to-end encrypted — see [Is InterChat end-to-end encrypted?](/help/privacy-safety/is-interchat-encrypted/) And anyone who receives something you send can screenshot, forward or save it, so share wisely.
