If notifications have stopped coming through, work through these four checks in order:

1. **Check your phone's settings.** Make sure notifications are allowed for InterChat in your phone's system settings, not just inside the app.
2. **Send a test notification.** In the app, open **Settings → Notifications** and tap **Send a test notification**.
3. **Check Quiet hours and mutes.** Make sure **Quiet hours** aren't on, and that the specific chat or community isn't muted — see [Muting a chat or community](/help/chats/muting-a-chat/).
4. **Check battery optimisation (Android).** Turn off battery optimisation for InterChat, since some phones pause background apps to save power.

If calls or Prayer Rooms specifically aren't ringing properly, see [Calls or Prayer Rooms not ringing full screen](/help/notifications/calls-not-ringing-full-screen/), which covers some extra settings on certain Android phones.
