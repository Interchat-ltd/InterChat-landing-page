---
title: Calls or Prayer Rooms not ringing full screen
summary: Android 14 full-screen permission, and extra settings on some phones.
keywords: call not ringing, full screen notification, tecno, infinix, xiaomi, oppo
order: 2
---

If a call or Prayer Room doesn't ring with a full-screen alert the way you'd expect, it's usually a phone setting rather than a problem with InterChat.

**On Android 14 and later:** make sure you've allowed **Full-screen notifications** for InterChat in your phone's settings. Android introduced this as a separate permission from regular notifications.

**On some phones** — including **Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi and Oppo** — the manufacturer adds its own extra settings on top of Android's. Also check and allow, for InterChat, in your phone's settings:

- **Show on lock screen**
- **Pop-up windows** (sometimes called "pop-up view" or similar)

These phones are more aggressive about restricting background apps by default, so InterChat needs explicit permission to interrupt the lock screen the way a phone call normally would.

Also check **Settings → Notifications → Prayer Rooms** in the app, and that the community isn't muted.
