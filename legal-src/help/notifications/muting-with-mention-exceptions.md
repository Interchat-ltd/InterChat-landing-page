---
title: Muting with @mention exceptions
summary: Muted chats stay quiet, except when someone @mentions you directly.
keywords: mute exception, mention notification, @mention
order: 4
---

Muting a chat or community stops its routine notifications, but InterChat makes one exception: if someone **@mentions you** by name, you'll still be notified, even though the rest of the chat is muted.

This means you can mute a busy community to cut down the noise, without worrying that you'll miss something addressed specifically to you.

For how to mute a chat or community in the first place, see [Muting a chat or community](/help/chats/muting-a-chat/).
