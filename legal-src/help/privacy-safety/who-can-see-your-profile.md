title: Who can see your profile
summary: You control your photo, bio, last-seen and stories — your name and username are always visible.

Your **username and name are always visible** to other InterChat users, so people can find and recognise you. Beyond that, you control who sees:

- Your profile photo
- Your bio
- Your last-seen time
- Your stories
- Whether you send read receipts

You can change any of these at any time from your privacy settings.

**In chats and communities**, the people in that chat or community can see what you post there. **Community admins** can see members' names, usernames, profile photos, roles and join dates — but not your private chats outside that community.

For more detail on exactly who sees what, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#who-can-see-what-you-share).
