You can report almost anything on InterChat straight from where you see it:

- **A message:** long-press it → **Report**.
- **A person:** open their profile → **⋯** → **Report** (you can also **Block** them).
- **A community:** Community info → **Report community**.
- **A story:** **⋯** → **Report story**.
- **A prayer request or comment:** **⋮** → **Report**.

Reports are **confidential** — we never tell the person who reported them. Content can be hidden automatically once it receives enough reports, until a moderator reviews it.

**What we might do,** depending on how serious it is and whether it's happened before: remove the content, send a warning, limit features for a time, suspend the account, permanently ban it, or report it to law enforcement where the law requires it or someone is at risk.

**If you think we got it wrong,** you can appeal within 30 days by emailing [safety@interchathq.com](mailto:safety@interchathq.com) with your username and what happened.

For urgent safety concerns, email safety@interchathq.com. **If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first** — InterChat can't call emergency services.
