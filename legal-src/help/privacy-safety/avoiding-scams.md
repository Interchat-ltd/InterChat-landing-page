title: Avoiding scams
summary: Never send money for "prayer fees" or to someone you haven't verified.
keywords: scam, fraud, prayer fee, fake ministry, money

---

Sadly, scammers sometimes target faith communities. InterChat has zero tolerance for this, but it helps to know what to watch for.

**Never send money because of:**

- A fake **"prayer fee"** or "seed offering" demanded in exchange for a blessing, healing or miracle.
- A fake ministry, charity or investment scheme.
- Pressure, threats, or a promise tied to payment.

Legitimate churches and ministries may share how to give, but never through pressure or a promise of a spiritual or financial return.

**Never send money to someone you haven't verified**, even if their profile or story seems convincing — profiles and messages can be faked.

**If you spot a scam,** report it: open the person's profile → **⋯** → **Report**, or the message → long-press → **Report**. See [Reporting and what happens next](/help/privacy-safety/reporting/) for what happens after. For anything urgent, email [safety@interchathq.com](mailto:safety@interchathq.com).
