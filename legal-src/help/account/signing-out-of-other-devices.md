---
title: Signing out of other devices
summary: Lost your phone, or think someone else has got into your account? Sign it out remotely.
keywords: sign out, lost phone, stolen phone, log out other devices, security
order: 3
---

If your phone is lost, stolen, or you think someone else has accessed your account, you can sign InterChat out on every other device without needing that phone in hand.

1. On a device you still control, open InterChat.
2. Go to **Settings → Where you're signed in**.
3. Find the device you want to sign out and remove it.

For good measure, also **change your password** straight after (see [I forgot my password](/help/account/forgot-password/), which works the same way even if you haven't forgotten it — just choose a new one).

If you believe someone else has actually accessed your account, tell us at [support@interchathq.com](mailto:support@interchathq.com) as well, so we can help keep it secure.
