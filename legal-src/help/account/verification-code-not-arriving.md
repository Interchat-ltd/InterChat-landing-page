If your verification code hasn't arrived:

1. **Wait a minute**, then tap **Resend code**. Codes can occasionally take a little while to arrive.
2. **Check your spam or junk folder**, if you asked for an email code.
3. **Check the address is spelled correctly.** You can change the email address or phone number right from the code screen if you spot a typo.
4. **Check your signal or connection**, if you're expecting a text message.

If you've tried all of the above and still haven't received a code, contact us at [support@interchathq.com](mailto:support@interchathq.com) and we'll help you get into your account.
