You can reset your password yourself, right from the sign-in screen:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Enter the 6-digit code we send to that email address.
4. Choose a new password.

You'll be signed back in once your new password is set.

**If you signed up with Google, Facebook or Apple,** you don't have an InterChat password to reset — just sign in with that service instead. See [Signing in with Google, Facebook or Apple](/help/getting-started/social-sign-in/).

**If you no longer have access to that email address,** contact [support@interchathq.com](mailto:support@interchathq.com) and we'll help you regain access.

If you think someone else has got into your account, also see [Signing out of other devices](/help/account/signing-out-of-other-devices/).
