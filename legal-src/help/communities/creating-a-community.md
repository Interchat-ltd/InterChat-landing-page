Anyone can create a community on InterChat.

1. Tap the new chat button, then **New community**.
2. Pick a template to get started quickly — **Bible study**, **Prayer group**, **Church** or **Friends** — or start from scratch.
3. Set the community's name, photo, description and privacy (public or private).
4. Invite people, either by sharing an invite link or adding people you know directly.

As the creator, you're the community's first **owner** — see [Roles in a community](/help/communities/roles-in-a-community/) for what that means, and what admins and moderators can do to help you run it.

You're responsible for setting rules that fit InterChat's [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html) — your community can be stricter than our guidelines, but it can't allow anything they forbid.
