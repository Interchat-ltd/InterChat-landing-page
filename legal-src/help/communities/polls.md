Polls let you ask a quick question in a chat or community and see how people respond, without everyone needing to reply individually.

Like other messages, a poll you send belongs to you, and it stays subject to the same [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html) as any other message in the chat.

Polls are a good way to settle on a time for a Prayer Room, agree on a Bible study topic, or get a quick read on how your community feels about something — without a long back-and-forth in the chat.
