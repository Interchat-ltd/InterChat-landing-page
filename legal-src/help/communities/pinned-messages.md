---
title: Pinned messages
summary: Admins can pin a message so it stays easy to find.
keywords: pin message, pinned, announcement
order: 7
---

Admins can pin an important message in a community — a meeting time, a welcome note, or anything worth keeping easy to find above the regular flow of chat.

Pinning is one of the tools available to a community's owners, admins and moderators as part of running it well. See [Roles in a community](/help/communities/roles-in-a-community/) for the rest of what admins can do.

If you're a member rather than an admin, you'll be able to see pinned messages, but pinning and unpinning is managed by the community's leaders.
