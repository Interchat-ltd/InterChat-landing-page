There are a few ways to join a community on InterChat:

1. **Browse.** Go to **Updates → Communities → See all** to discover public communities.
2. **Use an invite link.** Someone shares a link like `interchathq.com/join/…` with you — opening it on your phone takes you straight to the community's join screen.
3. **Ask to join.** Private communities need an admin to approve you before you can see what's inside.

Once you're a member, you'll see the community's chats, prayer requests and any Prayer Rooms it opens. Community rules are set by that community's owners, admins and moderators — see [Roles in a community](/help/communities/roles-in-a-community/).

Want to start your own instead? See [Creating a community](/help/communities/creating-a-community/).
