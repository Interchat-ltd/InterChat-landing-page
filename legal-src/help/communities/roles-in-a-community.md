Every community is run by the people who create and manage it — its **owners**, **admins** and **moderators**. They're responsible for setting its rules, keeping it welcoming, and acting on problems.

Admins can:

- Add or remove members
- Choose who can post
- Pin messages
- Manage Prayer Rooms

Community rules can be stricter than InterChat's own [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html), but they can never allow anything those guidelines forbid.

Communities marked **Official** are run by InterChat itself, rather than by an individual or church.

If a community's leaders are using their role unfairly — for example, pressuring members for money, or sharing members' information outside InterChat without permission — you can report it. See [Reporting and what happens next](/help/privacy-safety/reporting/).
