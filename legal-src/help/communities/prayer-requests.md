You can share a prayer request in a community or on the Prayer Wall, and others can respond with **"I prayed"** to let you know they've lifted it up, or post an update once it's answered.

**Posting anonymously.** If you'd rather not share your name, you can post a prayer request anonymously — other members won't see who posted it. For safety reasons, InterChat can still link an anonymous request back to your account internally, for example if it needs to be reviewed after a report.

Prayer requests are treated as a space of trust. Our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines.html) ask everyone not to screenshot, record or share someone's prayer request to embarrass or expose them, and not to post someone else's private details without their permission.

You can report a prayer request or comment that breaks these guidelines — see [Reporting and what happens next](/help/privacy-safety/reporting/).
