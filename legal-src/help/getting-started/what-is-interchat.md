InterChat is a faith-based messaging and community app, built so Christians (and anyone exploring the faith) can connect, pray and grow together.

Alongside the chats and group conversations you'd expect from a messaging app, InterChat adds things built specifically for faith life:

- **Chats and communities** for one-to-one conversations and groups, including templates for Bible studies, prayer groups and churches.
- **Prayer Rooms**, live voice gatherings where your community can pray together, take turns, and support one another out loud.
- **The Prayer Wall**, where you can share a prayer request (anonymously, if you'd rather) and others can respond with "I prayed."
- **Daily devotionals and the Verse of the Day**, plus a Bible you can read and listen to.
- **Streaks**, including a personal prayer streak and faith streaks you keep with a friend.
- **Stories**, for sharing testimonies, photos and encouragement that your community can see for 24 hours.

You own what you post, and you're in control of who sees it. See [Who can see your profile](/help/privacy-safety/who-can-see-your-profile/) and [Is InterChat end-to-end encrypted?](/help/privacy-safety/is-interchat-encrypted/) for the detail.

InterChat is free to use. **InterChat Plus** is an optional paid subscription with extra features — see [InterChat Plus](/help/plus/).
