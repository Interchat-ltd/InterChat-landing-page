---
title: Signing in with Google, Facebook or Apple
summary: Use an account you already have instead of a password.
keywords: google sign in, facebook login, apple sign in, sso, social login
order: 3
---

Instead of creating a password, you can sign in to InterChat with your Google, Facebook or Apple account.

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Facebook** or **Continue with Apple**.
2. Confirm the account you want to use.
3. Allow InterChat to receive your name, email address and profile photo from that service, so we can set up your InterChat profile.

We never receive your password for Google, Facebook or Apple — only the basic details you allow the service to share.

If you ever need to confirm a sensitive action (like deleting your account) and you signed up this way, we'll email you a 6-digit code instead of asking for a password, since you don't have one on InterChat.

You can use any of these sign-in methods the next time you open the app on a new device, as long as you use the same Google, Facebook or Apple account.
