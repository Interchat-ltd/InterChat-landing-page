InterChat links — like an invite (`interchathq.com/join/…`), a profile (`interchathq.com/profile/…`) or a Prayer Room link — are designed to open directly in the app. What actually happens depends on your phone:

- **App installed and verified:** the link opens the app directly; the website isn't involved.
- **App installed, but not verified yet, or opened from a browser:** you'll see an **Open in InterChat** page. On Android, this opens the app (or Google Play if it isn't installed yet). On iPhone, it uses InterChat's own link scheme.
- **App not installed:** the page explains what the link is (for example, "You're invited to join a community") and shows the App Store and Google Play buttons. For invite links, it also shows the code to paste into **Communities → Join with a link** after installing.

If a link still isn't opening the app the way you expect, try opening it directly in your phone's browser rather than from within another app (some apps open links in a way that skips straight to the browser).

Still stuck? Contact [support@interchathq.com](mailto:support@interchathq.com) with the link you tapped and what happened.
