Beyond reading, InterChat lets you listen to audio Bibles, sermons, podcasts and worship music, wherever you are.

This content is licensed from its publishers for you to listen to inside InterChat. As with text Bible translations, you can listen and share within the app, but you can't copy, download beyond the app's own offline features, or redistribute it, except as the publisher allows.

If you come across audio content, it's worth checking it reflects what you're looking for spiritually — InterChat's content is for encouragement, not a substitute for your own church and spiritual community.
