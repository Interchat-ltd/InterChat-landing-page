A faith streak is a streak you keep together with one friend, rather than on your own.

A day counts toward a faith streak when **you both** share one of these with each other, as a card, on that day:

- A verse
- A quote
- A sermon
- A podcast
- A reading plan

**What doesn't count:** ordinary text messages, photos, voice notes and prayers don't keep a faith streak going — it specifically needs one of the shared content cards above, from both of you, on the same day.

Faith streaks are a way to encourage a friend to stay consistent in their walk with God, with a bit of friendly accountability. Your own prayer streak works differently and doesn't need a friend — see [How does my prayer streak work?](/help/prayer-streaks-bible/prayer-streak/)
