---
title: How does my prayer streak work?
summary: Three daily goals keep your streak alive — reading, prayer and the Prayer Wall.
keywords: prayer streak, daily goals, streak, verse of the day
order: 1
---

Your prayer streak counts the days you've shown up consistently for your own time with God. A day counts toward your streak when you complete all three of these:

1. **Read today's verse** (the Verse of the Day).
2. **Finish a guided prayer.**
3. **Pray for someone on the Prayer Wall.**

All three need to be done on the same day for it to count — doing just one or two won't keep the streak going.

Your prayer streak is personal to you. If you'd like to keep a streak together with a friend instead, see [Faith streaks with a friend](/help/prayer-streaks-bible/faith-streak-with-a-friend/), which works a little differently.
