---
title: Getting a refund
summary: Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not InterChat directly.
keywords: refund, money back, reportaproblem
order: 4
---

InterChat Plus is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, so refunds are handled by them under their own policies, rather than by InterChat directly.

**On iPhone:** request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**On Android:** request a refund through your Google Play order history.

Where the law gives you a right to a refund, nothing about how InterChat Plus works limits that right.

If you're cancelling rather than asking for money back for a past period, see [Cancelling InterChat Plus](/help/plus/cancelling/) instead — you'll keep Plus until the end of the period you've already paid for.
