---
title: Cancelling InterChat Plus
summary: Cancel in the app, or directly in your App Store or Google Play settings.
keywords: cancel plus, cancel subscription, stop subscription
order: 2
---

You can cancel InterChat Plus at any time.

1. In the app, go to **Settings → InterChat Plus → Manage subscription**.
2. This takes you to your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, where you can cancel.

You can also cancel directly from your phone's App Store or Google Play subscription settings, without going through the app.

**You keep Plus until the end of the period you've already paid for** — cancelling stops the next renewal, it doesn't end your current period early.

Deleting the app, or deleting your InterChat account, **doesn't cancel the subscription by itself** — cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play settings. See [Deleting your account](/help/account/deleting-your-account/).

Changed your mind, or got a new phone? See [Restoring InterChat Plus](/help/plus/restoring/).
