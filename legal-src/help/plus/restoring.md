---
title: Restoring InterChat Plus
summary: Changed phones? Sign in and tap Restore purchases.
keywords: restore purchases, lost plus, new phone subscription
order: 3
---

If you've changed phones, reinstalled InterChat, or Plus just isn't showing as active, you can restore it without paying again.

1. Sign in with the **same account** you subscribed with.
2. Open the **InterChat Plus** screen.
3. Tap **Restore purchases**.

This reconnects your existing subscription from the App Store or Google Play to your InterChat account — it won't charge you again if you're already subscribed.

If it still doesn't show as restored, double-check you're signed in with the same InterChat account, and the same Apple ID or Google account, that you originally subscribed with. If you're still stuck, contact [support@interchathq.com](mailto:support@interchathq.com).
