InterChat is free to use. **InterChat Plus** is an optional paid subscription with extra features.

The exact features included are shown in the app at the time you subscribe, since they can change — so rather than list them here and risk being out of date, we'd rather point you to the most accurate place to see them: the **InterChat Plus** screen in the app.

One benefit we can confirm here: **InterChat Plus members don't see sponsored posts** from churches and ministries in the Updates tab.

Plus is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play and renews automatically until you cancel — see [Cancelling InterChat Plus](/help/plus/cancelling/).
