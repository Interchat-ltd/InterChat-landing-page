
# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** 15 October 2026

This policy covers the **website** at interchathq.com. The InterChat apps don't use browser cookies; how the apps handle information is described in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

## What we use

| Type | Why | Can you turn it off? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strictly necessary** | To make the site work, such as remembering a form you're filling in and keeping the site secure | No, the site needs them |
| **Preferences** | To remember choices such as your cookie choice | Yes |

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

**Third parties on our site.** Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons and scripts from jsDelivr and unpkg, which receive your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches them. The waitlist and contact forms send the details you type (such as your name and email) through EmailJS to our inbox. We don't use analytics or advertising tools on the website.

## Your choices

- Use the cookie banner on our site to accept or reject non-essential cookies, and change your mind at any time from the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working.
